### [1] [A Comprehensive Evaluation Framework for Conversational Home Energy Management Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00073)
*Wooyoung Jung*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 本文提出一个基于GQM方法的多维评估框架，用于评测LLM集成家庭能源管理系统在多轮对话中的表现，涵盖五大类23项指标，并用LLM-as-judge实现自动化评分，经人工编码验证后应用于970段对话、四种系统配置的对比评估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 家庭能源管理日益复杂，需要能结合用户背景、偏好与情境来引导其做出知情能源决策的先进系统。LLM集成的HEMS虽有前景，但以往研究多采用单轮或单任务评估，且以响应准确率作为主要指标，无法回答这类系统在真实使用中典型的长程多轮对话里是否具备有效交互能力。

Method: 基于Goal-Question-Metric方法论构建评估框架，划分为任务性能、事实准确性、交互质量、控制能力、系统效率五个类别，提出跨多轮对话的23项指标；采用LLM-as-judge流水线实现可扩展的自动评分，并通过三位受训人工编码员进行信度验证（经迭代式评分标准校准）。随后生成覆盖16个场景、5种用户画像的970段对话，对四种沿复杂度梯度排列的对话式HEMS配置（从使用原始能源数据的朴素LLM到多智能体HEMS）进行评估。

Result: 经迭代校准后，15项由LLM评分的指标中有12项达到强一致性（ICC ≥ 0.73），其中3项完全一致；其余3项因人工评分方差接近零，改用平均绝对误差（0.04–0.28）报告。评估结果显示该框架能在多个评价维度上区分四种系统配置，揭示其各自的优势与不足。

Conclusion: 本研究为对话式HEMS提供了一套可复现、多维度的评估方法论，能够全面评估系统在持续、情境感知场景下的表现，弥补了以往单轮、单任务评估的不足。

Abstract: The growing complexity in home energy management (HEM) demands advanced systems that guide occupants toward informed energy decisions reflecting their background, preferences, and context. Large language model (LLM)-integrated HEM systems (HEMS) have demonstrated promise, but previous studies relied on single-turn or single-task evaluations with response accuracy as the primary metric. Whether such systems deliver effective interactions across the extended multi-turn dialogues typical of real-world use remains an open question. This study introduces a comprehensive evaluation framework of LLM-integrated HEMS derived from the Goal-Question-Metric methodology, organized across five categories: task performance, factual accuracy, interaction quality, control capability, and system efficiency. A total of 23 metrics across multi-turn conversations are proposed and an LLM-as-judge pipeline is employed to enable scalable automated scoring. Its reliability is validated against three trained human coders: after iterative rubric calibration, twelve of the fifteen LLM-scored metrics reached strong agreement (ICC >= 0.73), three of them perfect, while the remaining three exhibited near-zero variance in human scores and are instead reported via mean absolute error (0.04-0.28). To demonstrate the framework's effectiveness, 970 dialogues -- 16 scenarios and five personas -- were generated and evaluated across four conversational HEMS configurations spanning a sophistication gradient, from a vanilla LLM with raw energy data to a multi-agent HEMS. The framework distinguished the four configurations across multiple evaluation dimensions, revealing their respective strengths and weaknesses. This study contributes to conversational HEMS by providing a reproducible, multi-dimensional evaluation methodology that comprehensively assesses sustained, context-aware system performance.

### [2] [Critsly and StudioCrit: An Artefact-Aware AI Critique Workspace and Simulation-Based Readiness Study for Design Education](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00085)
*Nizam Kadir*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 本文介绍Critsly——一个面向设计教育的"作品感知型"AI批评工作台及其建筑学工作室研究模式StudioCrit，通过模拟场景验证了从批评到证据的工作流可行性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 设计教育中的批评（critique）依赖对进行中作品的解读、意图表达以及将反馈转化为修改，这一过程难以被结构化记录与评估，因此需要一个能感知作品、组织批评并沉淀证据的工具。

Method: Critsly整合视觉画板、设计意图字段、引导式反思、多视角批评与行动计划；StudioCrit在此之上加入工作室/班级组织、角色权限、认知与建筑学分类、教育者分析及可导出证据。研究通过三个模拟工作室场景、一次使用50个一次性学习者账号的排练，以及一次加固排练进行验证。

Result: 三个模拟场景产出109条分类证据，其中85条归入高阶Bloom类别；50个账号排练产出56条证据，其中46条属高阶类别；加固排练记录50次完成会话、50次成功画板拉取及50次对学生访问分析的拒绝。

Conclusion: 贡献在于一个已实现的"批评到证据"工作流及其就绪程度的有界说明；这些属于软件与合成轨迹观察，并非学习收益或人类认知表现的测量，自动分类仍为暂定，尚未确立分类器准确率或评分者间信度。

Abstract: Critique in design education depends on interpreting work in progress, articulating intentions and translating feedback into revisions. This technical report presents Critsly, an artefact-aware AI critique workspace, and StudioCrit, its architecture-studio research mode. Critsly combines a visual board, design-intention fields, guided reflection, perspective-based critique and action planning. StudioCrit adds studio/class organisation, role-based access, cognitive and architectural classification, educator analytics and exportable evidence. The report consolidates implementation and simulation evidence recorded in a research project submitted in July 2026. Three simulated studio scenarios yielded 109 classified evidence rows, including 85 assigned to higher-order Bloom categories. A separate rehearsal using 50 disposable learner accounts yielded 56 evidence rows, including 46 assigned to higher-order categories. A subsequent hardening rehearsal recorded 50 completed sessions, 50 successful board pulls and 50 denials of student access to analytics. These are software and synthetic-trace observations, not measurements of learning gains or human cognitive performance. Automated classifications remain provisional, and the source report does not establish classifier accuracy or inter-rater reliability. The contribution is an implemented critique-to-evidence workflow and a bounded account of its readiness for further controlled evaluation.

### [3] [Three Pathways of Student-AI Interaction: Constraint-First Design for Higher-Order Thinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00338)
*Fatima T. Zahra*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 本文提出"学生—AI互动的三条路径"类型学框架与"下一层次教学蓝图"(NLTB)教学设计系统，并通过质性内容分析与GPT-5辅助编码检验其可行性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 学生与AI系统的互动方式可能决定该互动是支持还是取代批判性思维，因此需要一套描述互动模式的语言与促进高阶参与的设计方法。

Method: 提出三条路径类型学（被动浏览、直接提问、策略性对话）与三阶段NLTB教学设计系统；对某本科研究方法课程中随机抽取的50条学生—AI互动信息进行质性内容分析，两名人工编码者编码，并引入GPT-5作为第三位编码者。

Result: 人工编码者在路径层面一致率为68%（κ=.48），策略性对话识别一致率为80%；GPT-5给出相似的总体分布并新增了一个人工方案中没有的编码类别。主研究者分类中，路径1占46%、路径2占18%、路径3占36%；第二个描述性数据集以策略性对话为主，初步提示教学框架可能影响学生所选路径。

Conclusion: 三条路径框架与NLTB共同提供了描述学生—AI互动的语言和支持高阶参与的设计路径，但其教学效果仍需进一步验证。

Abstract: How students interact with artificial intelligence (AI) systems in educational settings may determine whether that interaction supports or displaces critical thinking. This paper introduces two contributions. The first is the Three Paths of Student-AI Interaction, a typological framework identifying three qualitatively distinct modes of student-AI engagement: Passive Review, Direct Question, and Strategic Dialogue. The second is the Next Level Teaching Blueprint (NLTB), a three-stage instructional design system intended to make Strategic Dialogue more likely. Qualitative content analysis of 50 randomly sampled student-AI interaction messages from an undergraduate research methods course was used to examine the typology. Two human coders achieved 68% path-level agreement ($κ$ = .48), with 80% agreement on Strategic Dialogue identification specifically. GPT-5, used as a third coder, produced a similar overall distribution and introduced a coding category absent from the human scheme. Path 1 (Passive Review) accounted for 46% of exchanges in the primary researcher's classifications, Path 2 (Direct Question) for 18%, and Path 3 (Strategic Dialogue) for 36%. A second, descriptively examined dataset contained predominantly Strategic Dialogue content, offering a preliminary indication that instructional framing may influence which path students take. Together, the Three Paths framework and the NLTB contribute a language for describing student-AI interaction and a design approach for supporting higher-order engagement.

</details>
